How to play

I

Type to attack

Type the hiragana on screen in romaji and your monster attacks.

II

Special move

When the gauge is full, press Space for a special move.

III

Drop-in battles

Practice against the CPU until another player joins your fight.

IV

Summon and raise

Summon companion monsters and raise them. Story and training modes too.
