About This Site

Bubblegum Game is a collection of free browser games handmade by "Bubblegum", a solo indie developer in Japan.

I'm a lifelong gamer who loves making games as much as playing them. These browser games include small JavaScript projects and larger Unity and 3D games; loading time depends on the game and your connection — if you like what you find, bookmark the site! I aim to update it about twice a month.
