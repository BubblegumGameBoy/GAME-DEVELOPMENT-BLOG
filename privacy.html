Privacy Policy

This page explains how Bubblegum Game (https://bubblegumgameboy.com/) handles personal information. Please read it before using the site.

Site Information

  • Site name: Bubblegum Game
  • URL: https://bubblegumgameboy.com/
  • Operator: Bubblegum (an individual)
  • Contact: contact form

You can learn more about the person behind the site on the About the Developer page.

Advertising

This site uses Google AdSense code and is applying to display advertising. When ads are available, Google and its partners may process data to deliver and measure them.

Third-party vendors such as Google use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site and other websites, in order to show ads tailored to your interests. Cookies, device identifiers, IP addresses and information about browsing activity may be processed. These identifiers can distinguish a browser or device even when you have not provided your name.

You can opt out of personalized advertising through Google's Ads Settings. For more on how third-party vendors use cookies and handle data, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics (provided by Google LLC) to understand how the site is used. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect traffic data. Traffic measurement can include pages viewed, device information and a browser identifier; it should not be treated as completely anonymous. This site waits for Google’s consent settings before loading Analytics. If a required choice is missing or denied, Analytics stays off.

If you would rather not have this data collected, you can disable cookies in your browser settings or install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. For details, please review the Google Analytics Terms of Service.

Contact Form Data

The contact form on this site (a Google Form) may ask for your name (a handle is fine), email address, and message.

Any information you provide is used only to reply to your inquiry, to send you any information you have requested, and to address game bugs. It is not used for any other purpose. Google processes form submissions as the form provider. I do not publish your message or contact details, and use them to handle the inquiry unless disclosure is required by law.

Your Rights Over Your Data

You can ask me what personal information I hold about you, and ask me to correct it or delete it. Send the request through the contact form and I will reply to the address you write from.

Visitors in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland

Google’s consent message lets eligible visitors consent, refuse or manage advertising and measurement purposes. Use Privacy & cookie choices in the site footer to reopen the applicable message and change or withdraw your choice. Google’s Ads Settings controls your Google advertising preferences separately. You may also contact us with data requests or raise concerns with your local data protection authority.

Visitors in California

Advertising partners may use data for personalized advertising. Where applicable, use the Do not sell or share link provided by Google’s privacy message to opt out. You can also contact us with requests about information held by this site.

Online Game Features

Online features send data to the services hosting each game. Leaderboards display submitted player names and scores. Dig Planet also shares player names, chat messages, discoveries and activity with other people in the same world. Avoid putting private information in a public name or chat message. For questions about game data, use the contact form linked above.

I do my best to keep the content and games on this site accurate, but information may occasionally be incorrect or out of date. I cannot accept responsibility for any damages arising from use of this site.

I am also not responsible for the information or services provided on external sites you reach via links or banners from this site.

Save storage differs by game. Some games save progress in your browser. Dig Planet stores its shared world and online player progress on its server; a browser-stored player identifier reconnects you to that progress. Clearing site data can remove a local save or the identifier used to access an online profile. Use Dig Planet's Transfer function before moving to a new browser or device.
