I Visited a Japanese Shrine Island in Pokopia and Found a Rayquaza Overhead

Nenrin Island packs a ring of Japanese buildings into one Cloud Island. Then you look up.

Bubblegum · Visited with the Mysterious Goggles · June 17, 2026

I walked through the shrine gate, happened to look up, and there was a Rayquaza.

I'd come to an island of Japanese architecture, and a huge Pokémon made of blocks was floating in the air above it. The contrast stopped me where I stood for a while.

The island is Nenrin Island, built by Ietaro (@ietarotktk), at the address GR3WBCC3. It's a ring-shaped corridor 55 blocks in radius, packed with Japanese buildings. The creator recommends visiting with the Mysterious Goggles, and all you need is the address.

Walking around inside

A shrine is the first thing in front of you, and the Japanese theme holds from the very first step. Past the shrine you head into the inner part of the island. Every few steps there's something new, and working out where to go next is half the fun.

Inside one building I found a tatami room. The interior matches the outside exactly. That level of commitment honestly impressed me, and I wandered around pointing the camera at everything.

Further in there's a many-storied building that looks like a five-storey pagoda. I couldn't tell if it was meant as a castle or a tower, and the scale of it is its own thing. I was surprised you could build this from the materials in Pokopia.

Look up, and there's Rayquaza

After walking the whole island, I glanced at the sky, and Rayquaza was there.

This is something you only get by going. A legendary Pokémon in block art, right above an island where everything else follows one Japanese theme. It's the opposite of keeping a theme consistent, and it works perfectly.

From the air you can take in the whole layout: Japanese buildings lined up along the ring, and Rayquaza floating above. After seeing it, the name Nenrin started to make sense to me. It means the rings in a tree trunk.

It works whether you're there to collect things for the 3D printer or just for building ideas. The goggles make it easy to drop in, so if you have a few minutes, take a look.

  • Island: Nenrin Island
  • Address: GR3WBCC3
  • Password: not needed
  • How to visit: Mysterious Goggles recommended
  • Highlights: Japanese buildings packed into a 55-block ring, and a block Rayquaza in the sky
  • Creator: Ietaro (@ietarotktk)
