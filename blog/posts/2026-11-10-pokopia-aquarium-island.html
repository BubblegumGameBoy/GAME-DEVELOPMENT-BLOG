Someone Rebuilt an Entire Aquarium in Pokopia, and It Beat My Gyarados Tank

HorseaAqua has statues at the door, a gift shop, tanks on three levels and staff rooms underground. I've built a Gyarados tank myself. This one is better.

Bubblegum · Visited in June with the Mysterious Goggles · November 10, 2026

It was raining at the entrance. A grand building, lined with Horsea statues. Inside, past the automatic doors, huge tanks rose up on both sides of me. This is a Cloud Island in Pokopia, and it felt exactly like walking into the Churaumi Aquarium in Okinawa.

  • Address: 56L1Y3NT
  • Island: HorseaAqua
  • Password: not needed
  • Creator: Ryune (@men_151)

It's an aquarium from the front door

The rain, the row of Horsea statues, the solemn building. Before you even step inside, your expectations go up.

There's even a gift shop. Details like this are what make a place feel real. It's adorable.

The entrance has working automatic doors. Go through and big tanks open out on both sides, just like the first moment inside a real aquarium.

The tanks: three levels, mud floors, and birds

The tanks are the star of any aquarium, and these are full of small touches.

The tanks are split into three levels. I'm impressed anyone thought of that.

Even the order they're lined up in feels like the route through a real aquarium.

Not every tank is clear water. Some have muddy floors, a different environment built on purpose.

There's even a section for birds. At this point it's a zoo. It goes well past what an aquarium has to be.

The Gyarados tank. I lost.

And there's a Gyarados. The size of its tank makes it the main attraction.

I've built a Gyarados tank of my own, and to be honest, it's nowhere near this. Total defeat.

From above, the shallow water along the beach looks amazing. I didn't know you could make shallows.

There's a whole world underground

It doesn't stop at ground level. Go down and the detail keeps going.

There's a power station in the basement. Maybe it's the machinery that keeps the aquarium running. Either way, the detail is incredible.

I think this is the staff area. Building even the behind-the-scenes spaces is the kind of thing that gets me.

Exploring down there, I somehow ended up inside one of the Pokémon tanks. Not a bad view, honestly.

A tip: behind the fields and orchard behind the Pokémon Center, there's apparently every kind of flower lined up. If you need flowers, photograph them there for the 3D printer.

I want to go back

When I visited in June, there was no way to build underwater yet. Since then, the August update added diving, and the Bubbly Basin expansion brought an underwater town and more water Pokémon. I keep wondering what this island looks like now, and I want to go back and see.

If you visit, the creator would probably love to hear what you thought. The goggles get you in quickly, so if you like aquariums, go and stand in the rain at the entrance for a minute.
