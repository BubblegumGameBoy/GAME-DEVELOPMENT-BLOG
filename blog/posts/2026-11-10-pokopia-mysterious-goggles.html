Pokopia Mysterious Goggles: How to Get Them, and the Official Islands to Visit

The goggles let you walk around anyone's Cloud Island with just the address. The official islands are the easiest place to start.

Bubblegum · Visited every official island on my first day with the goggles · June 17, 2026

The first day I had the Mysterious Goggles, I went round every island the official accounts had posted. They come with an address and a description, so it's the easiest way to start visiting Cloud Islands.

The official ones are picked by the people behind the game, and none of them were a letdown. Some are permanent and some are only up for a while, so here's how the goggles work and which islands I'd start with.

What the goggles do

The Mysterious Goggles take you to a Cloud Island in Virtual Mode, a sightseeing-only visit. Unlike joining from the PC next to the Pokémon Center, you can't build or gather, but you can walk anywhere you like. You can drop in at any hour, even when the owner is away, and if the island has no password, the address is all you need.

  • Walking around the island: yes
  • Photographing items with the camera: yes, and you can copy them with the 3D printer
  • Building, gathering, befriending Pokémon: no
  • Talking or working with other players: no

Photos you take of items come home with you, and you can copy them with the 3D printer in a Pokémon Center. Collecting things to print is the best use of the goggles.

How to get them

Raise the Environment Level of the first town, the Withered Wastelands, to 3, and the goggles appear in the PC Shop next to the Pokémon Center. You buy them with Pokémetal. If your level isn't high enough yet, keep working on the town first.

How to use them

  1. Open your bag, choose the Mysterious Goggles and press A.
  2. Enter the address of the island you want to see.
  3. You switch to Virtual Mode and can walk around freely.

To actually join an island and build, go through the PC instead: Link Play, then Cloud Islands. That way needs Nintendo Switch Online. I explain the difference in Palette Town vs Cloud Island.

The official islands

These were listed as permanent when I visited. Official islands do get added and removed, so if an address stops working, check the game's news screen.

Developer Island: PXQC G03S

Built by the development team. A giant Poké Ball-shaped building stands right in front of you, and up close its size is overwhelming. It feels like someone testing how far Cloud Island building can go.

EIKO Island: QBRK 7FVM

An island built around a kingdom and a throne, and the vertical building is beautiful. Everything is symmetrical, so it looks good in a photo from any angle. There's plenty to print, and plenty to borrow ideas from.

Yummy Yummy Island: 03X3 H83D

An island themed around food. The creator suggests touring it as Magnemite, so once you're in, try getting around with Magnet Rise.

The ones that come and go

Some official islands are only up for a limited time. While I was visiting there was a beach island with an easy, lived-in feel, and an IKEA collaboration island set out like a store showroom. Both have since closed.

Sitting on that beach chair, I felt like I was actually relaxing, which is a strange thing for a game to pull off. If a new limited island turns up on the news screen, go while you can.

After the official islands

Next, try islands players have built. An Only Up climb, an island with almost every item, a Japanese island with Rayquaza overhead. They're on another level from the official ones in their own way, and the addresses are all in my Cloud Island guide.
