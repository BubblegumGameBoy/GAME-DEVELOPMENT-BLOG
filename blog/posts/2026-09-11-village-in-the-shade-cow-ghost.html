Village in the Shade: The Red-Eyed Cow Ghost Quietly Costs You. How to Put It to Rest

It looks harmless and it's cute. It also stops your animals reaching full mood. Leave the barn door open and dig.

Bubblegum · From my own farm · September 11, 2026

One morning there were two cows on my farm. One was the cow I'd bought. The other had red eyes.

A cow ghost. No explanation, just standing by the barn as if it belonged there, and no way I could see to get rid of it.

Honestly, it was cute, so I left it for three days. That was a mistake.

It moves in, and you can't make it leave

It shows up at random, with no warning, some time after you build a barn.

And there's nothing you can do to drive it out. Talk to it and it's happy like a normal cow. Use tools on it and nothing happens. It just settles into your barn.

Leave it, and you quietly lose out

It looks harmless. It doesn't wreck your fields or take your animals. I didn't notice I was losing anything for three days either.

While the cow ghost is in your barn, the animals in that barn can't reach their highest mood. This is the speech-bubble icon on an animal's details.

I thought only the color changed, but the number of notes changes too. Green has one, yellow has three, and it goes up as the mood rises. With the cow ghost there, no amount of care got past green.

  • Visible damage: none. Fields and animals are fine.
  • Actual effect: animals in that barn can't reach full mood.
  • How to tell: the bubble stays green and the notes stop.
  • What it costs you in the end: your rating at the animal fair drops one grade.

If you don't care about the fair, there's no rush. The fair's rewards are pretty good, though, so you'll probably end up dealing with it.

If you're entering the fair, you sleep through the night before it, so deal with the ghost two nights before at the latest. Animals in the chicken coop aren't affected, so if you're entering a chicken, don't worry. It comes back even after you've sent it on, so check your farm again as the fair approaches.

How to put it to rest

Forget the first step and the rest is wasted.

  1. At night, leave the barn door open.
  2. Late at night, climb the narrow stairs north of your house.
  3. Dig up the mound at the cow ghost's feet with the hoe.

1. Leave the barn door open

If it's closed, the ghost doesn't come out at night, and climbing the stairs just takes you to an empty clearing in the woods. I did exactly that once. I walked the night path, stood in an empty clearing, thought about it for a while, and went back.

2. Climb the narrow stairs north of your house

It's north of your house, up a narrow staircase beside the chicken coop.

The stairs are narrow and easy to miss. They wind up between rocks from beside the barn, and the first time you won't think it's a path.

At the top, you come out into a small clearing surrounded by trees. In the daytime there's really nothing there.

Listen for mooing. If you lose your bearings, follow the sound uphill. That's how I first noticed it: I was on my way to fish at the mine and heard a cow somewhere above me.

3. Dig up the mound at its feet

Go to the same place at night and the cow ghost is standing there, with a small mound of earth at its feet.

Dig up the mound with the hoe and it's put to rest on the spot. Talking to it or petting it does nothing.

What you get changes each time

It looks like a one-off event, but the cow ghost keeps coming back, and you get something different each time.

TimeWhat you get
1st3 big bones and the livestock whistle
2nd5 big bones
3rdA cow skull, wall-mounted night furniture
4th onward5 big bones, or the cow skull

If you want the wall skull, you'll need to go round three times. The bones are a nice bit of money too.

What the whistle does

Blow it and your animals nearby come to you. Handy when one wanders off, or once your farm gets big.

A bug to know about

An early version of the game had a bug: after finishing the cow ghost event, removing every barn that can hold cows or goats could make the game impossible to progress. It's been fixed in updates, but if you're playing without updating, keep at least one finished barn when you rearrange your farm. Tearing them all down to rebuild is the riskiest move.

Dig it up early

After three days of leaving it, my animals never reached full mood the whole time. It's not a loss you can't recover from, but if I'd entered the fair without noticing, I'd have lost without knowing why.

And when I finally dug, the cow ghost I'd thought of as a pest just left behind a whistle for calling my animals. A red-eyed cow standing in the yard is a sight I wouldn't mind watching a while longer, though.
