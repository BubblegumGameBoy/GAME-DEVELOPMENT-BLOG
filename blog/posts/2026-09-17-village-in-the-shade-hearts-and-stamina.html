Village in the Shade: How to Get More Hearts and Stamina (The Pool Guardian and the Fox Shrine)

I reached winter with three hearts. Max hearts come from one place only, and stamina from a fox you follow at night.

Bubblegum · From my own playthrough · September 17, 2026

I got all the way to winter with three hearts. Early on, the nights scared me, so I barely walked the village after dark. Then I realized how much I'd been missing, and I regret it a little.

Only the guardian's scale raises your max hearts

The item that adds a heart is a scale from the guardian of the deep pool. Not food, not equipment, not leveling up. Just that. And the only one who gives it is the guardian that lives in the pool east of the village.

To call the guardian up, you throw cursed things into the pool. Cursed things means cursed crops and cursed fish, and as long as you farm and fish normally, you'll never have any.

Step 1: pick up mud seeds in the village at night

The seeds you need aren't sold in shops. Walk the village late at night and pick up the glowing things on the ground. Some of them are these small purple mud seeds.

You can also get them from a clay-like ghost. It crawls around for a while, buries three mud seeds in the ground and disappears. Dig them up with the hoe.

Night is when the spirits are out. Rather than heading far into the mountains, I think it's best to circle the village and pick up whatever you can.

Step 2: plant them and wait for a cursed crop

Plant a mud seed and a random crop of that season grows. Sometimes it's a normal vegetable, sometimes it's a misshapen cursed crop. You can't choose.

There are 18 cursed crops in all, changing with the seasons, from onions and strawberries in spring to Chinese cabbage and daikon in winter. You can't cook with them either, so throwing them in the pool is their only use.

If the season changes while one is growing, it dies, so avoid planting near the end of a month.

Careful: cursed crops take hearts from you when you harvest them. Crops meant to raise your max hearts lower your hearts. If you cut a lot at once with only a few hearts left, you'll go down, so do it in the morning after a full night's sleep.

Step 3: pay Konno 1,000 yen

Talk to Konno while carrying a cursed crop and you'll be taught how to handle it. It costs 1,000 yen.

Only after this conversation can you throw things into the pool. When I just stood on the pier holding crops, nothing happened. You only need to talk to Konno once, to unlock it.

Step 4: throw them into the pool at night

Throw from the pier that sticks out into the pool.

Hold the cursed thing in your hand and throw it at the water. You can throw in the daytime, but the guardian only rises late at night.

A few seconds later, something huge with whiskers floats up from the bottom. When its face filled the screen, it was three times bigger than I'd expected and I made a noise.

From the second time, the price shoots up

Only the first time costs one. After that, each throw asks for more.

Five rounds gets you five more hearts, but that takes 35 cursed things. Mud seeds don't always become cursed crops, so you'll dig up even more seeds than that. From the second round on, save up and go in one trip.

Hearts only go up for the first five rounds. After that, every 15 gets you other rewards, such as the Rain Whistle. You can check how many the next round needs at a mailbox-like piece of furniture on the waterfall pier.

For bulk, break giant cursed crops with the pickaxe

Growing them one at a time, you'll stall around the fourth round. To get a lot at once, wait for a crop to grow giant.

Crops occasionally grow huge. Hit a giant cursed crop with the pickaxe and ten things drop at once, cursed crops included. Ten for the effort of one. From the third round on, whether you get one of these decides how many days it takes.

For a crop to grow giant, keep fences, paths and furniture out of the 5×5 squares around the mud seed. A giant crop takes up that whole space, so other crops planted nearby disappear.

Fishing helps too. Fish late at night and you sometimes catch cursed fish. I confirmed six kinds: pale chub, crucian carp, river crab, amago trout, ayu and frogs. You can throw them in the same way, so when your fields are full, fishing is the quicker way to add to the count.

Stamina goes up at the fox shrine

The white bar just under your hearts is stamina. Running, tilling and chopping trees use it up, and when it's empty you can't do anything.

Walk the village at night and a fox appears. It runs when you get close, but keep following and you'll reach the fox shrine.

The shrine moves every season

The shrine appears somewhere different each season. Where the fox shows up is fixed too, so if you can't find it, walk that area late at night.

SeasonWhere the fox appearsWhere the shrine is
SpringNear the general storeUp the side path by the clinic
SummerBehind Kisuke's houseAcross the crossroads bridge
AutumnNear the building companyUp the stairs beside the village head's house, on the path above the water mill
WinterNear the hunters' stationJust before the stairs down to the mountain fishing spot

The shrine asks for that season's offerings. In winter there are two, wild plants and vegetables. Bring what it asks for and you get fried tofu as a reward, and your max stamina goes up. There's one fried tofu per season, so stamina goes up four times in all.

If you're farming, the vegetables take care of themselves

The vegetable offering asks for whatever your fields grow that season. In winter, Chinese cabbage, daikon and spinach. Just farming normally fills one of the two lists for you. Wild plants don't grow in fields, so you'll gather those around the village. I keep a storage box just for wild plants, since villagers ask for them too and this game makes you hoard everything.

The offerings change each season, and winter vegetables are winter crops, so if the season changes you start over. You don't have to finish in one night, though. Whatever you've offered carries over. From the second visit, the fox drops one item you haven't offered yet in front of the shrine, so visiting the fox is the shortcut for whatever you're missing.

I wish I'd known all this before winter. Walk the village at night early, and those first three hearts won't last as long as mine did.
