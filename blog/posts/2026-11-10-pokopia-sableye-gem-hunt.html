Pokopia Sableye's Gem Hunt Guide: The Fastest Dream Island for Red Crystals

A limited-time event from spring 2026. I compared the Dream Islands so you know where to go when it comes back.

Bubblegum · Played through the 2026 event · April 29, 2026

Sableye's Gem Hunt ran in Pokopia from April 29 to May 14, 2026. You collect red crystal fragments on Dream Islands and take them to the Pokémon Center, where Sableye trades them for camping and adventure-themed items you can't get any other way.

Getting one of everything takes 64 fragments, which is two or three days if you visit a Dream Island daily. If it comes back, here's how I'd play it, including which Dream Island gave me the most fragments for my time.

  • To take part: at least one Pokémon Center rebuilt.
  • It doesn't happen on Cloud Islands.
  • No Nintendo Switch Online needed.

How it works, in four steps

1. Find Sableye near the Pokémon Center

During the event, Sableye shows up near the Pokémon Center. Talk to it and become friends. If it isn't there, go to another area and come back.

2. Go to a Dream Island

Show a doll to Drifloon and it takes you to a Dream Island. Any Dream Island works. You can only go to one a day, so keep it up daily.

3. Break the red crystals

The red crystals are underground on Dream Islands. Each one you break gives four fragments. Holding Y as Magnemite picks up a whole cluster at once, which is faster. If you want to keep one as decoration, you can collect it whole.

4. Trade at the Pokémon Center

Take the fragments back to the Pokémon Center and talk to Sableye to trade. You can trade for the same item more than once. If your bag is full, the item drops on the floor and you can pick it up later.

The oil lantern and jewel wall decoration only cost two each. The sleeping bag, cooking pot, adventure kit and tent kit cost ten, so if you want more than one of those, collect extra.

Which Dream Island gives the most

I visited each Dream Island a few times and compared. The Eevee doll's island was the most efficient. It's small, so you spend less time getting around, and fragments per second were far higher than anywhere else.

For fragments alone, go to the Eevee doll's island. If you want other materials at the same time, pick the island for those: Arcanine's has gold, Dragonite's has wood.

The crystal clusters you pick up whole as Magnemite also work as decorations, so they're worth keeping.

Keeping Sableye after the event

During the event Sableye appears outside the Pokémon Center. To keep it in town afterward, it needs a habitat.

Its habitat is the treasure-hunting set, three items: the adventure kit, the map and compass, and the treasure. All three come from this event, so collect them while it's running.

Sableye likes the dark. Put the habitat inside a cave or somewhere roofed and dim and it'll be more comfortable. A campfire or lamps nearby make it too bright, so it doesn't sit well in a camping habitat.

What I built with the rewards

The rewards suit camping and cave builds perfectly. I made a campsite, and an oil lantern inside a cave looks great. Put the cooking pot over a campfire and you get a proper camp meal, like in the picture. Surround the tent with something like a canyon and it'd feel even more like a real trip.

If the gem hunt comes back: get the three habitat items first, go to the Eevee doll's island for speed, and keep a few whole crystals for yourself.
