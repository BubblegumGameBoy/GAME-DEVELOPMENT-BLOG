Village in the Shade: How to Beat the Red Tentacle (Just Keep Cutting Grass)

A glowing red tentacle hides under the village's grass. Most of the night is cutting grass while small things chase you.

Bubblegum · From my own nights in the village · September 23, 2026

On the night the glowing red tentacle comes out, almost everything you do is cutting grass.

I went without anything to heal with, and when I finished it off at 2 a.m., I had less than half my hearts left.

How to beat the tentacle, in short

  • When you enter the village at night, a tentacle bursts out. That's your signal to switch to the sickle.
  • Cut every patch of grass in the village to find the glowing red main body.
  • If grass with legs, or a big tentacle lined with teeth, shows up, move away. Contact costs hearts.
  • Hit the main body three times with the sickle and it dives. Cut grass to find it again. Do this three times.
  • Don't leave the village partway. If you do, you start over.

1. A tentacle bursts out as you enter

Go into the village at night and the screen closes in from top and bottom, and a glowing red tentacle shoots out of the ground. If you see that, it's a tentacle night.

The main body then hides somewhere in the grass that covers the village. You find it with the sickle, so switch to it now.

2. Cut grass, all of it

The main body hides under grass, and there's no way to tell its patch from any other. Its spot is random, so you cut patches one after another as you see them.

Cut the right one and the main body pokes out of a hole at your feet with a red glow.

There's no trick to spotting it, so it comes down to cutting a lot. This is the part of the night that takes longest.

3. Walk away from the grass with legs

While you're cutting, grass with mouths and legs swarms out and chases you. They look harmless, but if they touch you, you lose hearts.

You can't drive them off with the sickle, so when they appear, move away. Run for a while and they're gone.

There's grass wherever you run to, so you can keep cutting there. Running and searching get done together.

4. And don't touch the big toothed tentacle

Every so often a big tentacle grows out of the ground too. It's much larger than the main body, with rows of teeth along the inside.

This isn't the main body. It's an enemy that turns up to get in your way. Touching it costs hearts, so keep your distance when you see it.

5. Three hits and it dives. Three times.

When you find the main body, hit it three times with the sickle. It dives back into the ground, and you go back to cutting grass to find it.

The grass creatures keep chasing you while you cut. When the screen flashes red, that's a hit, and each hit takes a quarter of a heart.

Make it dive three times and the tentacle is beaten. Nine cuts in total.

For me, the second appearance came at 12:30 a.m. and I finished it off at 2. That hour and a half was nothing but cutting grass and running from the small ones. The longer the grass-cutting goes on, the more often you get hit.

6. Leave the village and you start over

On the lantern ghost's night, leaving the village and coming back in was the best escape when chased. Do that on a tentacle night and you lose your progress, dives and all.

Even when your hearts get low, you can't reset outside the village. You'll have to heal inside it.

At 2 a.m. my stamina was still nearly full. Only my hearts had gone down. If you bring food, bring something that restores hearts rather than stamina.

7. Its real name is nozuchi

The tentacle's proper name is nozuchi. It's on the description of its wooden tablet.

In folklore, the nozuchi is a snake yokai with no eyes or nose, only a mouth. It's also another name for the tsuchinoko, which is surely why one of its drops is a tsuchinoko doll.

You'll often read that it appears "from summer." I met it on spring day 21 of my second year, so I read that as "not before your first summer."

To call it instead of waiting, use the tablet. It's sometimes among the glowing drops in the village at night. Offer it at the spirit shelf you get from an event in your first spring, go to sleep, and the nozuchi appears that night. On nights you sleep through to morning, the tablet isn't used up.

8. What you get

  • Night furniture: a tsuchinoko doll, and a mud bowl. Sometimes dropped when you beat it.
  • A lost book: lying on the path to the right of Hasumi and Sumire's house, only on this night.

The book is only there while the tentacle is out, so pick it up while you're cutting grass.

What I got the first time was a scarecrow.

No night furniture that time. A scarecrow, set in a field, lets that scarecrow's season's crops grow even out of season, so it wasn't a bad draw.

Two hours of cutting grass, and I got a scarecrow for my field. For a night of farm work, that makes sense.

What a tentacle night wears down is your hearts, not your stamina. If you're not bringing healing, raise your max hearts first and a few hits won't panic you.
