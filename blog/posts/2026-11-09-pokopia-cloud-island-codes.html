Pokopia Cloud Island Codes: 10 Player-Made Islands Worth Visiting

Addresses, screenshots and what each island is good for, from a full Only Up climb to an island with almost every item to copy.

Bubblegum · I visited and photographed every island · November 9, 2026

One island I visited in Pokémon Pokopia had a shrine at the entrance, tatami rooms, a tower stacked floor on floor, and, once I happened to look up, a Rayquaza made of blocks floating in the sky.

Anyone can build a Cloud Island, anyone with the address can walk in, and the best ones go far beyond anything the story asks you to make. These are ten I've walked around myself, with their addresses, what each one is good for, and a couple of screenshots so you know what you're getting into. One of them is mine, and it's a prison.

Addresses stop working when an owner closes or rebuilds an island. Everything here worked when I wrote it, and I swap out the dead ones when I find them.

How Cloud Islands work

A Cloud Island is Pokopia's online space. It sits completely apart from the towns in the story, and up to four players build it up together from nothing. You don't need an invitation to visit one. If you know the address you can walk in at any hour, whether the owner is online or not.

Raise the Environment Level of the first town, the Withered Wastelands, to 2 and Link Play opens up on the PC in the Pokémon Center. That took me about an hour from a new game. Each island has its own shared Pokédex with more than 200 entries to fill, so this isn't a side mode you finish in an evening.

There are two ways in. Link Play from the PC lets you build and gather like a resident. The Mysterious Goggles, sold in the PC Shop once the Environment Level reaches 3, let you look around an island in Virtual Mode without changing anything, which is all you need for the islands below. Either way you need a Nintendo Switch Online membership, and you can bring one Pokémon with you.

Copying things you find on other islands

Anything you can photograph, you can print.

  1. Open the camera with the − button.
  2. Press Y to switch to object mode, and check that the screen says you're photographing an item.
  3. Take a picture of the furniture or item you want.
  4. Feed the photo to the 3D printer at the Pokémon Center and pay the Pokémetal it asks for.

Photos also work as pictures on signs and tablets, which is why the sign island further down is more useful than it sounds. Printed copies don't count toward the collection of things you dig up, though, so those you still have to find yourself.

1. Nenrin Island: GR3WBCC3

This is the island from the top of the page. Ietaro built a circular corridor 55 blocks in radius and packed it with Japanese buildings: tatami rooms, the shrine, a tower that stacks up like a five-storey pagoda. The Japanese theme starts before you have taken a step, and it never lets up. The Rayquaza is in the sky above all of it, and on its own it's worth the trip.

Visit with the goggles. Creator: @ietarotktk.

2. PokonlyUP!: NDFQ8XN5

If you know Only Up, the game where you climb a tower of junk and lose everything when you fall, you already know what this is. Someone rebuilt it inside a Cloud Island, twenty stages stacked from the ground to the height limit.

There are house rules. You can use Rollout, Surf and Waterfall. Glide is allowed after a fall under certain conditions, and Magnet Rise is banned. I could not believe someone had gone this far with a Cloud Island. It's the one island on this list that's a game in itself, so go and see how far you get.

Creator: @penguin_uts1.

3. HorseaAqua: 56L1Y3NT

A row of Horsea statues lines a grand building. Walk through the automatic doors and tanks rise up on both sides of you. Ryune rebuilt an entire aquarium, and it felt like walking into a real one.

The tanks are split across three levels. Some have mud floors, there's a bird section, and the shallow water you see from above has been faked with real care. There's even a power station in the basement. The Gyarados tank is the star.

If an update ever adds more water to build with, this island is going to get even better. Creator: Ryune (@men_151).

4. Mel's Island: 6DXLPD6F

This one is all set pieces. Walk one lap of the island and you keep finding things: the fountain, flowers that turn out to be Pokémon, a Pikachu laid out block by block in pixel art, a pillar of water.

One lap gives you more than enough to stock up on things to print. Creator: @Mel_shota.

5. Sign Island: CS8PGM1V

An unusual idea: an island of signs from the Pokémon world. An Eevee café, a Poké Mart, a Pokémon Center, Professor Oak's lab, and more.

You don't come here to look around. You come to take pictures. Photograph the signs in object mode, print them, and your own buildings get lettering that actually belongs in the setting. If you want your island to feel like part of the Pokémon world, this place is a treasure chest. Creator: @kuroi_wioru.

6. Mixed Build Island: JNWQY9Y1

The name is honest. Every building style is in here, with no single theme holding it together, and that freedom is the fun of it. There's a residential street with a slightly Dutch feel, and you never know what's round the next corner.

7. Rocket Warehouse: V3F7MYHL

The idea is an island with every habitat in the game, and the catch is that all of them are underground.

Behind a Team Rocket door there's an elevator with a trick to it. I'm not going to describe it. Ride it without knowing.

8. Popo Island: RRWF8H35

The first thing you see is the castle, and the scale of it's overwhelming. I didn't know Pokopia's building tools could make something this finished. Parts of the island around it are still being worked on, which gives it the feel of a place being built right now, and I want to come back and see what has changed.

9. My island, the prison: TX699DP0

In Pokopia, a Pokémon placed inside certain enclosures can't move. I used that to build a prison. Friends who visit say either "this is hilarious" or "this is horrible", never anything in between.

There's a basement too, and the way down is near the middle of the island. I started on it and looked up to find several hours had gone. I've since built a second prison in the sky. The whole story, including the jail in the sky, is here.

10. Item Complete Island: CGPV5RN4

If you're visiting for the 3D printer, this is the best island on the list. Walk in with the goggles, take photos, and you can copy almost every item in the game.

If you only visit one

Make it Nenrin Island, walk in through the shrine, and remember to look up.
