Village in the Shade · Night guide

Village in the Shade: The Snow Woman Hands You a Baby. Where the Three Graves Are

You can't refuse her. But you don't have to carry the baby around the whole village looking for the grave.

Bubblegum · From my own snowy nights in the village · September 13, 2026

Walking the village late at night, I was stopped by a woman in a white kimono.

She was oddly polite about it. I wasn't given a choice, though. Before I knew it, I was holding a baby.

What she's asking you to do

The game never shows her name, but a book you can find in the village calls her the woman of the snowy night. A woman in a white kimono who appears on snowy nights: in other words, a yuki-onna, the snow woman of Japanese folklore.

She asks one thing. Take the baby she's handed you to its father. The father is already in his grave, so what you're looking for is a gravestone.

  • When: snowy nights in the village, only on days when the weather icon at the top right is a snowman
  • Can you refuse: no
  • What to do: carry the baby to its father's grave
  • The grave: one of three places, at random, all along stone walls
  • While carrying the baby: you move slowly and can't pick things up
  • In your way: little snow children all over the village
  • If you fail: drop the baby and the woman chases you with a terrible face
  • To retry: leave the village and it resets

The easy way: find the grave first

Carrying the baby slows you down, and the grave is in one of three random places. Searching the whole village while carrying it is the hardest way to do this.

You also can't pick anything up while you're holding it, and on a night out in the village that alone is a waste.

  1. Put the baby down on the ground where you are.
  2. Walk around the village light-footed and find the grave. Pick things up on the way.
  3. When you find the grave, leave the village from the spot closest to that gravestone.
  4. Come back in the same way. You start again holding the baby, right by the grave.
  5. A few steps and you're there. Put the baby down.

The moment you put it down, the woman's face changes and she comes after you, but you're light without the baby and can outrun her. Keep dodging while you do one lap of the village. Once you know where the grave is, you've basically won.

You don't need to go back for the baby you put down. Leave the village and come back in, and you start holding it again, so don't worry about where you left it during your scouting.

Leave from the spot nearest the grave. When you come back in, you start from there, so you only carry the baby a few steps instead of crossing the whole village at a crawl.

It's fine to treat the first try as scouting. Rush to deliver it and you'll get lost at a crawl and get caught.

The three possible graves

It's always one of these three.

  • 1: down and to the left of the village head's house, along the river, beside the water mill
  • 2: by the house just above the library
  • 3: by the jizo statues, near the clock and the clinic

Go around the outside and you can check all three. They're all against stone walls. Searching the middle of an open square won't find them, so walk with your eyes on the walls.

Don't let the snow children see you

Snow children pop up all over the village. They're little baby ghosts in straw capes, and if they see you while you're carrying the baby, they chase you.

Most of them are in the middle of the village. Rather than cutting straight across, follow the outside from beside the general store and you'll run into fewer of them.

If you take damage, you drop the baby, and the woman chases you just as if you'd put it down yourself.

Lay the baby down, and the father appears

Put the baby down in front of the grave and it disappears. Right away, another ghost appears.

The father.

At the same moment, every snow child in the village vanishes. The roads you'd been tiptoeing around are suddenly just snowy roads again, which makes picking things up much easier.

What you get

A piece of night furniture drops in front of the grave. There are two, a snow rabbit and a snowman. I got the snow rabbit.

The books about her

Two Lost Books tell her story. I found the first one around the house down and to the right of the village head's house, marked blue on the map above. Put them on the library shelves and you can read them.

It comes down to scouting first

Search while carrying the baby, and it goes slow, can't pick anything up, spotted, dropped. Once that starts, there's no recovering.

Put it down first, find the grave while you collect things, leave from near the grave and come back in. Splitting the job like that makes it a different level of difficulty.

You can't refuse her. But you don't have to carry the baby around the whole village either.
