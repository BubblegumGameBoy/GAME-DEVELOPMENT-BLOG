Someone Rebuilt Only Up Inside Pokopia, and I Climbed It

PokonlyUP! turns a Cloud Island into a climbing game: furniture footholds, rules on signs, and twenty stages to the height limit.

Bubblegum · Climbed to the top · April 15, 2026

Touring Cloud Islands, every so often I find one that makes me say "wow" out loud. PokonlyUP! is one of them.

Someone recreated a completely different game inside Pokopia. You get to play a game inside the game.

What Only Up is

Only Up is a brutal climbing game where falling means losing your progress. A huge space is filled with real-world furniture and objects, and you use them as footholds to keep going up. Fall, and you're back at the start, or a long way down. That rule is a big part of why people got hooked on it. My favourite streamer played it, and watching someone climb all the way up on a live stream and then slip off is impossible to look away from.

The Pokopia version has the same picture: furniture as footholds, all the way up. Pokopia's Cloud Islands let you build that high, and the creator designed the whole thing as a climbing game. I take my hat off to them.

Read the sign first

Right at the entrance, a sign explains the rules. Using Pokopia's own sign feature to explain the rules of a game is a fun idea on its own.

  • Allowed: Rollout, Surf, Waterfall
  • Allowed only to get back after a fall: Glide. Not for getting ahead.
  • Banned: Magnet Rise

It isn't just a climb. Each move has places where you're meant to use it and places where you can't. Working out which move gets you past each section is the game.

Climbing

You can't relax even at the start. There are narrow paths to edge along, and the question of how far you'd drop if you fell keeps you tense.

There's a stretch where you climb across beds. Climbing on top of furniture is exactly the feel of the original, and it's obviously on purpose.

Some parts have you jumping across treetops. You never walk on trees in normal Pokopia, so you don't know where the landing edge is, and it's nerve-racking.

The part that scared me most

Partway up, a sign appears saying the rolling form is allowed. A sign there means you can't get enough distance without Rollout. This was the part that scared me most. You have to land while still rolling, and I nearly went over several times.

Past the hard part, there's a switch that presses when you land on it. It isn't only climbing. Gimmicks like this get mixed in, and they get denser the higher you go.

Then it turns into Ninja Warrior

Then moving platforms show up, and suddenly it's Ninja Warrior. This goes beyond Only Up and becomes a proper stage of its own. You have to time your jumps to the moving footholds. Honestly, how deep does this game go?

I made it. How long it takes, and how hard it is

I made it to the top, and it took about 10 to 15 minutes. Reaching the summit felt like a real achievement.

To be fair, it only took 10 to 15 minutes because Glide was allowed after a fall. Without it, every fall would send you back down, and it could easily have taken over an hour. I'm looking forward to someone streaming it, but I hope they ban Glide.

As for difficulty: it's tough. Landing spots and Ditto's jump distance are tuned carefully, with spots that only just reach and spots that don't. You can't get through by jumping vaguely. Every stage makes you think about how you move.

I'd never seen anything on a Cloud Island work this well as an actual game. The creator, @penguin_uts1, has done something genuinely impressive.

Island details

  • Island: PokonlyUP!
  • Address: NDFQ8XN5
  • Password: not needed
  • Time to clear: about 10 to 15 minutes, with Glide after falls
  • Difficulty: tough, with carefully tuned landings and jump distances
  • Highlights: Only Up recreated across twenty stages, with rule signs, gimmicks and moving platforms
  • Creator: @penguin_uts1
