Village in the Shade: The Floating Lantern Ghost and Where the Five Purple Lanterns Are

A paper lantern drifting by itself. Break five purple lanterns you can only see by flashlight, without being seen.

Bubblegum · From my own nights in the village · September 22, 2026

Walking the village late at night, I saw a paper lantern floating by itself. Even after I learned that breaking some stone lanterns would end it, I couldn't work out where in the village those were, and I wandered around until dawn.

How to deal with the lantern ghost, in short

  • Go into the village at night carrying a flashlight and a pickaxe.
  • Circle around the outside of the village so the lantern doesn't spot you.
  • Shine your flashlight to find the purple stone lanterns, and break them with the pickaxe. Five of them.
  • If you're spotted, leave the village and come back in.
  • After five, a bigger lantern appears in the square. Break that too.

1. Without a flashlight, you can't even see them

You need two things, the flashlight and the pickaxe.

The flashlight is what matters. The purple lanterns don't show up unless you light them. Look for them by eye in the pitch-dark village and you'll never find them, so walk with your light pointed ahead.

As for the pickaxe, switching to it after you find a lantern is too late. In the few seconds it takes to switch, the lantern ghost drifts over. Even the time spent with the tool wheel open adds up.

2. Where the five purple lanterns are

  • Northwest: in the garden of a house east of the hunters' station
  • North: just south of the water mill, across the river
  • East: east of the clock tower, by the row of jizo statues
  • West: below the building company, hidden among the trees. This is the hardest one to find.
  • South: beside the street stall, near the bridge

They look like a single purple crystal stuck into the ground. As I said, they're invisible until you light them, so when you get near a circle, sweep your flashlight around on the spot.

3. Don't let the lantern see you

The lantern ghost doesn't attack. It just drifts along a set route. Your job is to get to each stone lantern and back without it noticing you. Think of it as a stealth game, and move from cover to cover.

Its route passes close to the stone lanterns. Or rather, I think it circles near them because they're there. You can't avoid that if you're going to break them, so wait until it drifts away before you go in.

4. Go around the outside

The last, bigger lantern appears in the square in the very middle of the village. Since the goal is in the center, the shortest route is to go once around the outside and then drop into the middle.

Start around the street stall in the south, then pick up the west, northwest and north ones, and finish with the east one by the jizo. In that order, you come out in the square.

The night is shorter than you think. Find five by flashlight, slip past the lantern ghost, and get back to the square. Take your time looking around and it'll start getting light. The first time, I wandered through the middle of the village before heading for the outside, and ran out of time.

5. If you're spotted, leave the village

Being spotted doesn't do anything to you right away. The trouble comes after. Wander the village for a while and a different ghost appears and chases you. It's fast, so carrying on looking for lanterns while it's after you is basically impossible.

Take one step out of the village and come back in, and everything resets. Lanterns you've broken don't come back, so rather than being chased around, leaving once is the quicker way to finish.

Going out and back in does eat into the night, though. You don't have time to do it again and again, which is why circling the outside from the start is the right call.

6. Finally, the big lantern in the square

After the fifth one, the game tells you something seems to have appeared.

In the square, there's a lantern a little bigger than the others. Break it with the pickaxe the same way. The moment it breaks, the lantern ghost and the chaser both vanish from the village, and you get two pieces of night furniture, an eyeball lantern and a stone monument.

Afterward I walked the same paths again. No lantern floating anywhere. By the jizo and under the clock tower, it was just a dark village again.

The wooden tablet for this one gives the lantern's owner a name: tenome, the folklore spirit with eyes in its palms.

Half of what makes nights hard is probably max hearts. With three, one accident is enough to send you home. Raise them first and the nights get easier.
