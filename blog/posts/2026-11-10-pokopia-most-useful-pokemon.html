The Most Useful Pokémon in Pokopia: Tinkaton Changes How You Build

After the story, I moved onto my Cloud Island. Some Pokémon change the pace of building just by being there.

Bubblegum · From my own Cloud Island after finishing the story · November 10, 2026

I finished Pokopia's story, and since then I've more or less lived on my Cloud Island. That's where I noticed how much difference the right Pokémon makes. Some of them change the pace of building completely, just by being there.

These are the five I found most useful, with how to make their habitats.

1. Tinkaton: building, much faster

Building on a Cloud Island takes a lot of back and forth, from gathering materials to placing furniture and buildings. With Tinkaton around, buildings finish dramatically faster. Its Engineer specialty means it helps with construction like nothing else in the game.

Once you've befriended it, bring it to your Cloud Island before anything else.

Habitat: the Oversized Dumping Ground. Three iron beams or columns, one waste bin of any kind, one tire and one microwave oven. The microwave comes from a random recipe, or from the Poké Balls on the floating island you reach with the Dragonite plush.

2. Haxorus: logs, then lumber

Haxorus is the only Pokémon that leaves logs lying around, with the Litter specialty. It can also cut those logs into lumber with its Chop specialty, so on its own it handles everything from supply to processing. Put it near a storage box and it'll bring the logs over and turn them into lumber automatically.

3. Volcarona: thread, and fire

Volcarona drops thread and can also burn things. Fire is needed in a lot of places, like making bricks or using a bread oven, so being able to burn is quietly important. Play online for long and you end up going to Dream Islands to smelt huge amounts of iron, and you can never have too many furnaces.

Habitat: either a shady flower garden, or a hot magma habitat made from one hot rock and two magma.

4. Blissey: stones, and trading

On a Cloud Island you tend to run out of stone. Blissey keeps dropping small stones, which mostly solves that. It can also trade, and putting it at a trading post keeps items moving. Stone supply plus trading makes it a great fit for Cloud Islands.

Habitat: the cute series of furniture, placed together.

5. Vespiquen: honey for furniture

Vespiquen trades the honey you've collected for furniture, like a natural-style sofa. Honey is also what brings Ampharos, so trading with Vespiquen every day is efficient. It's most useful while you're still working through the story towns, but it keeps paying off as a way to collect furniture.

Habitat: any flower-garden type, somewhere with flowers in bloom.

A few more worth having
