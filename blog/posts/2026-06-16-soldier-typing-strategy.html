Soldier Typing strategy, with the actual numbers from my own game files

I built this game, so the numbers below are not estimates from playing. They are the values in my own source.

Most strategy guides for a game this small are guesswork dressed up as advice. I do not have to guess, so here is the real thing: every boss stat, every unit stat, and the one design decision that should change how you play.

The thing nobody would guess: the enemies never get stronger

There is a line in the source that reads, more or less, waveMult = 1, with a note next to it saying enemies stay at Wave 1 strength throughout.

That is not a bug and it is not laziness. Goblins in Wave 5 have exactly the same 80 HP and 2 damage as goblins in Wave 1. The trash never scales.

Your side does, slightly. From Wave 4 onward, every unit you spawn gets 10% more HP. Nothing else changes.

So the difficulty curve in this game is carried entirely by the bosses. If a wave feels hard, it is the boss doing it, not the mob. Play accordingly: you are never fighting a rising tide, you are fighting five specific monsters with five specific problems.

Look at the HP column and notice that it does not climb. It goes 500, then 3,600, then down to 3,200, then down again to 1,600, then explodes to 9,600.

Which means Wave 2 is the real difficulty spike

The jump from Wave 1 to Wave 2 is 7.2 times more boss HP. Nothing else in the game comes close to that as a single step. If people bounce off this game, that is where it happens, and it happens because they are still playing it like Wave 1 was teaching them to.

Wave 2 is where you have to stop casually typing and start actually spawning at volume.

The Demon Lord is a trick, not a wall

Wave 4 looks terrifying and has less than half the HP of the fight before it. 1,600, versus Vel's 3,200.

What it has instead is 24 damage per hit, double Vel's, and the slowest movement in the game at 0.006. It is a glass cannon that lumbers. It deletes whatever it reaches, and it takes a long time to reach anything.

The counter falls out of those two numbers: do not feed it. Its damage only matters if your units walk into range one at a time, and its slowness means you have time to build up before contact. Wave 4 punishes trickling and barely notices a swarm.

God is the only genuine HP check

9,600 HP, 42 damage, and a reach of 110, which is the longest in the game by a clear margin. Vel reaches 75. God reaches 110, so it starts hitting your line noticeably earlier than anything else.

This is the fight the whole game is actually about. Everything before it is teaching you to sustain spawn volume so you can survive this one.

The archer is better than it looks, and dies to a stiff breeze

Archers out-damage soldiers, 15 to 11, and they reach 140 versus the soldier's 34. That is roughly four times the range. An archer standing behind your line contributes for the entire fight without ever being touched.

The catch is 22 HP. A hobgoblin hitting for 6 kills an archer in four swings; the Demon Lord's 24 damage erases one instantly. Archers are free damage exactly as long as something else is standing in front of them, and worthless the moment they are not.

Soldiers do less damage each but have 35 HP and move at 0.046, faster than either enemy type. They are the wall. Archers are the payload.

The Hero is worth eleven soldiers

120 damage per swing against the soldier's 11, plus 9,999 HP, which in practice means it does not die. It also moves at 0.055, the fastest thing on the field, so it reaches the front before your other units do.

When it shows up in Wave 4, the fight changes character completely. It is not a small buff.

What all of that adds up to

  • Wave 1 is a tutorial. 500 HP. Whatever you do works. Do not learn your habits here
  • Wave 2 is where the game starts. 7.2x the HP of Wave 1. Switch to typing for volume, not for accuracy points
  • Wave 3 is easier than Wave 2 on paper. 3,200 versus 3,600, and Vel is faster at 0.015, so it closes the distance sooner. It feels harder than it is
  • Wave 4 is a positioning puzzle. Low HP, huge damage, very slow. Build up, then commit. Never trickle
  • Wave 5 is a stamina test. 9,600 HP and the longest reach in the game. Nothing clever here, just sustained volume
  • Short words beat perfect words. A spawned unit contributes whether or not the word was impressive. Speed of spawning is the resource

None of that is visible from inside the game, which is exactly why I wrote it down.

Soldier Typing is free in the browser. It is Japanese typing practice, so words are typed in romaji, which makes it a decent workout if you are learning.
