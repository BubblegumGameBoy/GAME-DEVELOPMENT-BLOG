Village in the Shade: How to Beat the Wind God and Thunder God (Shoot the Turtles)

Giant gods over the village on a storm night. You don't shoot them. You shoot eight turtles, and dodge the lightning.

Bubblegum · From my own storm nights in the village · September 26, 2026

On a stormy summer night, a giant wind god and thunder god appear over the village.

You don't aim at either of them. Shoot the see-through turtles wandering the village with your bow: four red turtles beat the thunder god, and four blue turtles beat the wind god.

When I finished both, it was 4:30 in the morning, with thirty minutes left before time ran out.

How to beat them, in short

  • A storm day is wild from the morning, so go out that night with your bow.
  • Shine your light on a faint round glow and there's a turtle. Shoot it.
  • Four red turtles beat the thunder god. Four blue turtles beat the wind god.
  • Watch the ground for lightning warnings. When balls of light form a ring around you, stand still.
  • Count what's left by the thunder god's drums and the wind god's wind bag.
  • Time runs out at 5 a.m. If you only beat one, it carries over to the next storm.

1. You know in the morning

On a storm day, the rain and wind are strong from the morning, and the weather icon at the top right shows thunder, so you know during the day that the gods will come that night. You'll use your bow and your flashlight.

The one surrounded by drums is the thunder god, Raijin. The one holding a bag of wind is the wind god, Fujin. They never attack at the same time. They take turns.

2. Light up the round glow, and shoot

The turtles wander all over the village, somewhere different every time. From a distance they're just faint round glows, but get close and shine your flashlight and you'll see a turtle's shape.

Near a turtle, you hear something like a heartbeat. Dash around the village, and when you hear it, search that area carefully.

When you find one, raise your bow. Two dotted lines extend in the direction you're aiming. Shoot when the turtle is between them.

You can't tell a turtle's color until you get close or hit it. Hit a red one and it becomes the thunder god's turn, and lightning starts falling. Hit a blue one and it's the wind god's turn, and the wind blows.

3. Read the lightning. The wind only pushes.

The thunder god has three attacks, and each one that hits takes a heart.

When small bolts fall one after another, a reddish-black shadow appears first where each one will land. Keep moving and don't step on the shadows, and they won't hit you.

A big blue circle on the ground warns of a big bolt. It falls the moment the inner circle grows out to meet the outer one, so get outside the circle before then.

When balls of pale light line up in a ring around you, do the opposite and stay where you are. The ring may spread outward or shrink inward, but if you're in the middle, neither hits you.

On the wind god's turn, strong wind just pushes you, and it doesn't cost hearts. Sometimes the wind starts while lightning shadows are still on the ground, though, so don't get pushed onto one.

4. Count the turtles left by the drums and the bag

You can tell how many you've beaten by looking at the thunder god's drums and the wind god's bag.

The thunder god has four drums around it, and each red turtle you beat breaks one. I didn't notice this at all while playing.

The wind god's bag works the same way. It has four bulges, and each blue turtle you beat deflates one.

5. Time's up at 5 a.m. One at a time is fine.

There are eight turtles in all, and between dodging lightning and searching, 5 a.m. comes fast.

If you only beat one of the gods before morning, it isn't wasted. On the next storm night, only the one that's left appears.

One reward, though, only comes from beating both in a single night. If you run out of time, a soft reset before you go home lets you redo the night from midnight.

6. What you get

  • Thunder god hanging scroll (wall night furniture): beat the thunder god with four red turtles
  • Wind god hanging scroll (wall night furniture): beat the wind god with four blue turtles
  • A strange statue (night furniture): beat both gods in one night
  • A Lost Book about the wind god, the thunder god and the turtles: picked up behind the village head's house on a storm night

The scrolls only go on walls, but the statue works in the yard or indoors. I hung the two scrolls side by side on a wall and put the statue in my yard.

7. The book that's only there on storm nights

On storm nights, a Lost Book is lying in the village too, behind the village head's house.

Miss the storm night and you can't pick it up until the next one, so stop by while you're hunting turtles. Put it on a library shelf and you can read it.

Every lightning hit costs a heart. You can raise your max hearts with a separate event, so do that before a storm night and you can take a few hits and keep going.
