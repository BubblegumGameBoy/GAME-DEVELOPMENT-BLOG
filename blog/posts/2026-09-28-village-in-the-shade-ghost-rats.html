Village in the Shade: The Ghost Rats, All 30 Spots, and How to Make Cheese for the Well Rat

The rats don't attack. They want cheese. Thirty of them, thirty old coins, and one Well Rat at the end.

Bubblegum · From my own nights in the village · September 28, 2026

Walk up to a pale, glowing, ghost-like rat in the village or the mountains at night, and the game just asks: "Rat?"

It doesn't attack, so dealing with it only means giving it cheese. Getting to the point where you can make cheese is the long part.

How to deal with the rats, in short

  1. At night, find a pale glowing rat in the village or the mountains.
  2. Walk up holding cheese and give it.
  3. Follow the rat when it runs off.
  4. Dig where it disappears with the hoe, and pick up an old rat coin.
  5. When you have 30 coins, throw them into the old well.

1. Find the rats (30 spots)

A few rats appear each night in the village and the mountains. There are 30 spots in all, and which ones are used changes every night.

The 30 rat spots, numbered from north to south.

Spots dug: 0 / 30

Tap a circle on the map or a number to check it off. Checks are saved in this browser, so mark the spots you've dug and you'll see which are left.

A spot you've dug never has a rat again, but spots you haven't dug will show up again on other nights. There are a lot of them, so don't stress about it. If you see a rat, give it cheese.

2. Hold the cheese and give it

Walk up holding cheese and the X button changes to "give." One per rat, so you need 30 in all.

3. Follow the rat

A rat that's taken the cheese runs off somewhere. Follow it without losing sight of it.

From giving the cheese to the rat disappearing took about thirty minutes on the game clock. Rats vanish at 5 a.m., so if you give cheese after 4:30, it may disappear before it gets there.

Rats in the mountains sometimes run into hunting grounds you haven't unlocked yet. Then you can't follow, and you don't get the coin. Mountain rats are safer once the hunting grounds are open.

4. Dig where it disappeared

When the rat vanishes, it leaves a round mark on the ground. Dig there with the hoe and an old rat coin comes up.

After you dig, check that spot off on the map above.

5. Throw 30 coins into the old well

The old well is at the end of a side path off the road from the village to the waterfall fishing spot, a dead end. Throw in 30 old rat coins and you get a piece of night furniture: the Well Rat.

Making cheese

  1. Unlock the mill at the shrine and build it at the workbench.
  2. Dig rock salt at the quarry and grind it into salt in the mill (5).
  3. Dig iron ore from floor 40 of the quarry and smelt it into iron ingots in the furnace (2).
  4. Unlock the milk processor at the shrine and build it with the salt and the ingots.
  5. Put in goat milk and wait a day.

1. Unlock the mill at the shrine

First you need a mill to make salt. You learn it at the shrine at the crossroads for an offering of magatamas and a bronze mirror. You build it at the workbench from 20 stone and 10 wood.

2. Dig rock salt and grind it

Rock salt comes out of stones you break with the pickaxe on the first floor of the quarry, where you mine ore.

Put rock salt in the mill and in a day it's salt. The milk processor needs five.

3. Make iron ingots in the furnace

The other material, iron ingots, comes from the furnace. Put in five iron ore and one coal or charcoal as fuel, and in twelve hours you get one ingot.

The milk processor needs two, so that's ten iron ore. Iron ore doesn't appear until floor 40 of the quarry.

4. Unlock and build the milk processor

You learn the milk processor at the shrine too, for another offering of magatamas and a bronze mirror.

The materials are the five salt and two iron ingots you've made.

5. Put in goat milk and wait a day

Put goat milk in the milk processor and in a day it's cheese. Cow's milk only makes cream, so it has to be goat milk.

Goats go on sale at the stall in the square after you've entered the animal fair once. A kid costs 4,000 yen, grows up in ten days, and gives goat milk every day after that.

The shrine also lets you unlock an upgraded milk processor that takes up to three at once.
