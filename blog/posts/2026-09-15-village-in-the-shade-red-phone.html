Village in the Shade: The Red Phone Event, Step by Step (Go to the Phone Booth First)

A red phone you keep losing by morning. It's an event, and it starts at a phone booth at the edge of the village.

Bubblegum · From my own nights in the village · September 15, 2026

There was a red mobile phone lying in a field in the village. Every time I picked it up, it was gone again by morning. It turns out that's not a glitch. It's an event.

How the red phone event goes, in short

  1. Answer the ringing phone in the phone booth. This starts the event.
  2. On a later night, pick up the red phone lying in the village.
  3. When it starts ringing, don't answer. Run to the phone booth.
  4. Answer the red phone inside the booth and you get a broken phone.
  5. Take the broken phone to Sazanka in the daytime.

Picking up the red phone first does nothing. The phone booth comes first.

Step 1: answer the phone in the booth

The booth is at the edge of the village. Follow the road in front of the street stall straight to the right and cross a bridge.

It's away from the center, so the first time it looks like a dead end, but it's there.

The pay phone inside is ringing. Answer it. That starts the event.

Step 2: pick up the red phone

From the day after, a red phone starts turning up in the village at night.

  • Where: the village at night, at a random spot
  • How to find it: it rings when you get close
  • If you leave it: it's gone from your bag the next day

Its spot is random. Both times I picked it up, it was in the field by Yuta's house, but I've also seen it elsewhere. Follow the sound. It rings as you get close, so even if you can't see your feet in the dark, walking toward the sound gets you there.

If you pick it up and do nothing, it's gone from your bag the next day. I lost it this way more than once. Get to step 3 the same night you pick it up.

Step 3: when it rings, don't answer. Run to the booth.

A while after you pick it up, the red phone starts ringing. For me it rang while I was walking near the bridge, so I suspect that area triggers it.

Don't answer it there. Running all the way to the booth with it still ringing is more reliable. Answer it and the ringing stops, and you have to wait for it to ring again.

When you reach the booth, answer the red phone.

Inside the booth, you're shut in. The glass on all four sides turns deep red and you can't see out, and after a while the red phone rings. The door won't open until you answer. Some of the words that come through are too broken to read.

If you're being chased

From the day after you start the event, a woman with wild hair appears in the village at night. If she spots you, she follows, and her touch hurts.

And while the red phone is ringing, a white figure comes closer.

  1. The phone rings.
  2. Use the red phone.
  3. Answer it.
  4. The figure that was closing in disappears from the screen.

That makes the figure back off, but the ringing stops too, so you'll have to redo step 3. If you can make it to the booth, run without answering. Think of answering as the escape for when you're cornered.

If you meet another spirit while the phone is ringing, you can get boxed in with nowhere to go. The eyes that fill the screen also come out at night, so clearing the area around you first isn't a bad idea.

Steps 4 and 5: the broken phone goes to Sazanka

When the booth is done with you, you get a broken phone. Carry it to the general store in the daytime.

You don't need to hand it over. As long as you're carrying it, Sazanka spots it and trades you a plush toy, a piece of night furniture.

There are two plush toys, Osugi and Onimaru. Both are Sazanka's prototypes for a village mascot. You can put them on desks and shelves, and outdoors too.

I put them out

Pick up a cursed phone, get shut in a phone booth at midnight, hear the voice of the dead, get chased by a woman with wild hair. And what you get for all that is a prototype of the village's mascot.

A red parasol in the snow, with both of them beside it. Once they're out, you forget where they came from. Osugi is a cedar tree, Onimaru is an ogre, simple designs that look exactly like their names.

Every spirit at night rewards you with night furniture, so each one you deal with makes your house and yard a little busier. Pick up the cursed thing, hear the story, put it on display. That's most nights.
