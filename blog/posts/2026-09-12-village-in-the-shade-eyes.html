Village in the Shade: The Eyes That Fill the Screen, and How to Beat Them

Black haze from the corners of the screen, then dozens of eyes. Light alone won't stop them. Off, on, and one arrow.

Bubblegum · From my own nights in the village · September 12, 2026

Walking the village a little before 2 a.m., I noticed black haze seeping in from the corners of the screen.

I ignored it, and it kept spreading. Leave it, and in the end it fills the whole screen at once.

Eyes. Dozens of them.

If you just want to get away, walk up to one of the glowing stone monuments in the village and the haze disappears. To beat it, wait for the screen to fill, switch your flashlight off once, and from the eyes that scatter and chase you, shoot the one with a target pattern with your bow. Shining the flashlight on them forever won't do it.

What these eyes are

They appear when you go into the village at night, in any season.

If the screen is completely covered and you do nothing, you take damage and collapse. The first time, I had no idea what to do and just sat in a bush until 5 a.m.

  • Where: the village at night
  • When: all year
  • Warning sign: black haze seeping in from the corners of the screen
  • If ignored: the screen is covered, you take damage and collapse
  • You need: a flashlight and a bow

You can escape without fighting

First, the option that doesn't involve fighting, for anyone without a bow yet.

There are glowing stone monuments in the village. A hollow in the rounded stone glows pale blue, so you'll know one when you see it.

Get to one of these before the haze covers the screen and the haze clears for a while, and the eyes stop chasing you.

Remember where the monuments are and stop by now and then, and you can walk the village safely. If you don't want to fight, that's enough. If you can't find one, flick your flashlight on and off.

This only clears it for a while. The eyes don't go anywhere, and the haze comes back from the corners before long. To get rid of them, beat them like this.

How to beat it

The order is unusual.

  1. When the screen is covered, turn your flashlight off once.
  2. The eyes scatter and chase you one by one.
  3. Turn the flashlight back on.
  4. Equip your bow.
  5. Among the eyes chasing you, find the one with a target pattern and shoot it.

Off first, then on again

I assumed shining light on them would make them disappear, and kept the flashlight on them. That never ends it.

Switch the light off while the screen is covered and the clump of eyes breaks up and comes after you one at a time. The target only appears once they've scattered, so turn it off first. After that you can turn it back on, and it actually makes aiming easier.

Light slows them down

Shine the flashlight on them and they chase you more slowly. If you're surrounded, light them up first to slow them, then aim. You can ignore the other eyes. As long as you can light up the target, you're fine.

This is the target

This is the hardest part. The scattered eyes all look alike, but one has a different pattern.

A normal eye has just a crescent of light along the edge of its pupil, and the middle is solid black. The target has rings layered outward from a white dot in the center, exactly like an archery target. It almost looks like it's swirling.

When you find it, shoot it, and you're done.

What you get

The first thing it dropped for me was a scarecrow.

The moment you hit it, every scattered eye vanishes. After a screen full of eyes, the empty ground looked oddly wide.

There are also two pieces of night furniture, a black crystal and an obsidian prayer bead string, which turn up after you've beaten it a few times. It also drops that season's scarecrow.

It appears all year, so you'll run into it if you wait, but if you want to beat it repeatedly for the furniture, use its wooden tablet. In the tablet's description it's called hyakume, "a hundred eyes."

You find the tablet lying around during night exploration. Put it on the spirit shelf you get from an event in your first spring, go to sleep, and it appears that night. On nights you sleep through to morning nothing happens, but the tablet isn't used up.

A book you may only find while it's out

Two lost books are connected to the eyes. You don't get them by beating it. You pick them up in the village.

  • One is on the riverside path heading north toward the hunters' station.
  • The other is near the smoking area in the square, and only appears while the eyes are out.

Judging by when I found the second one and what it says, it's very likely only there while the eyes are active. Put books you find on the library shelves and you can read them.

Two things to remember

To escape, go to a glowing monument. To beat it, switch off, switch on, and shoot the target. It might be the simplest spirit in the game to deal with.

Next time the corners of your screen go dark, you don't need to sit in a bush until 5 a.m.
