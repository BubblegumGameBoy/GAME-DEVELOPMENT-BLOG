Updates

I keep tweaking the games to make them more fun to play.
Here's a log of the updates so far — new bosses, sound effects, balance changes, UI improvements, and more.

Entries record changes on their original dates. Older balance values and language descriptions can differ from today's games. Use the current player guide and each game's instructions for the version available now.

September 10, 2026 · Player guide and site update

A clearer first visit, from choosing a game to pressing Play

The new player guide compares all ten games by device, language and first goal, with a four-word typing warm-up. Game pages now explain the controls before loading the game and provide an Exit fullscreen button that returns to the guide without restarting your session.

Dig Planet opens in English from this site. The keyboard games have English controls or entry help; Japanese practice words and some story text remain. Reviewed strategy guides link to the game version used for their calculations. The Mythos Slayer page also shows the redesigned interface in a new September 10 screenshot.

September 2, 2026 New Game Soldier Typing 2

Soldier Typing 2 is out!

The sequel to Soldier Typing. Same keystrokes, six waves instead of five, and some of those waves are won by surviving the clock rather than by clearing the field. There is also a boss that is not on the map, reachable two different ways, and a route that only opens after you have already won once. At launch, the interface was Japanese. English entry instructions and help were added in September; story and battle text still include Japanese. Copy the displayed romaji prompts to type. Play →

August 20, 2026 New Game Dig Planet

Dig Planet is out!

One planet, and everyone who opens the page digs the same one in real time — your holes stay in their world and theirs are already there when you arrive. 26 million voxels, six layers of rock, ore veins worth up to 60x a swing, and something buried at the core that a pickaxe will not go through. The launch interface was Japanese. The current version offers English and Japanese, opens in English from this site, and supports mouse or touch controls. Play →

July 4, 2026 New Game Cthulhu Typing

Cthulhu Typing is out!

A typing-meets-summoning battle: every word you finish typing summons a soldier that auto-attacks a daily boss from the Cthulhu Mythos (43 in all). It has a daily leaderboard. The current limit is one successful clear per day; a failed attempt can be retried. Run length depends on how the battle goes. Play →

June 26, 2026 New Game Piano Typing

Piano Typing is out!

A rhythm-meets-typing game: falling tiles show you the next key to press, and playing at your own pace lets you perform classic melodies. There's no timing judgment, so it also makes for great home-row practice. Play →

June 23, 2026 Balance Mythos Slayer

Prestige bonus bumped up to 5x

This June announcement summarized the bonus as 5x. Current rule, checked September 10: Rebirth uses 1 + 0.4 × highest boss cleared in the current cycle. Clearing Boss 10 gives a factor of 5; Boss 20 gives 9. The factor multiplies your existing permanent bonus, so it is not a fixed 5x at every reset. See the current Rebirth guide before deciding when to reset.
