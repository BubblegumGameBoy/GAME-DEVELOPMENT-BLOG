Monster Typing

Type Japanese in romaji to make your monsters attack. A free online typing battle game, played in your browser.

The game runs inside a post on my blog, so every player's save stays in one place.

Play on the blog

The post and the game's screens are in Japanese. You type the words in romaji, on the keyboard you already have.

Monster Typing is an online typing battle game. Japanese words appear in hiragana, you type them in romaji, and your monster attacks. Other players can drop into your fight in real time, and you summon and raise companion monsters as you go. It's free and runs in your browser.

How to play

  1. Type the hiragana on screen in romaji, and your monster attacks.
  2. When the gauge is full, press Space for a special move.
  3. Until someone else shows up, you practice against the CPU. When another player arrives, they drop into your fight.
  4. Summon companion monsters and raise them. There's a story mode and a training mode too.

On a PC you play with the keyboard. On a phone, battles work with flick input.

If you're learning Japanese

I made it for Japanese players, but it works as Japanese practice too. You read hiragana over and over, fast, because the fight depends on it. There's also a practice mode for kanji and Japanese history. Since you type romaji, the letters on the keyboard you already have are enough.

The monsters and their world

There's a field guide to 19 monster lines, with where each one lives, how its body works and how big it gets, plus notes on the world map and the regions the story visits. Both are in Japanese for now.
