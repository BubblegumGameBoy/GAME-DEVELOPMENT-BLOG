Popcorn Typing

Japanese Typing PC

A snappy idle typing game where every keystroke pops popcorn to a satisfying rhythm. Great romaji typing practice, too. A keyboard game, for PC.

Best played on a PC with a keyboard. The game interface is in Japanese - you type hiragana words using romaji (Latin letters), so it's great practice if you're learning Japanese.

An idle typing game where every key you press sends popcorn popping and piling up. Type to fill your container and it keeps upgrading: cup, bucket, truck, building, whole city. Even when you stop typing, your facilities keep roasting popcorn on their own, so it quietly grows while you're away. A nice companion for typing practice, too.

I wrote this one, so the numbers below come from the balance file rather than from playing and guessing.

How to Play

Click the game once to give it focus, then just type the romaji prompts. You upgrade using the on-screen buttons (keyboard keys 1, 2, 3).

  1. 1. Level Up: increases how much popcorn each keystroke makes. The basic upgrade for strengthening your manual typing
  2. 2. Buy Facilities: add auto-roasters like frying pans, microwaves, and factories. This is what determines your idle production
  3. 3. Variety Research: ranks up your popcorn variety, sending your per-keystroke output shooting up. Reach for this when you stall out

The formula behind a keystroke is variety × level × combo, and finishing a whole word pays a separate bonus of five times the normal rate plus a large burst. So the game rewards completing words rather than mashing at them, which is the one place where it is quietly a typing trainer rather than a clicker.

The Combo

Consecutive correct characters build a multiplier, and a single mistake sets it back to zero.

10 in a row×2
25×3
50×4
100×6
200×10

The gap between 100 and 200 is the interesting one. Two hundred characters without a single miss is a long way, and the reward for getting there is nearly double, which is deliberate: it is the only point in the game where accuracy is worth more than speed.

Four Varieties

Variety research is the biggest single jump available to you, and there are only four steps.

  • Ordinary Corn — 1 kernel per character. Where everyone starts
  • Caramel Corn — 15 per character, for 2,500
  • Truffle Salt Corn — 350 per character, for 250,000
  • Solid Gold Corn — 9,000 per character, for 20 million, and it has no ceiling. Every further research doubles it

Seven Facilities

Facilities are the idle half, and they are deliberately modest: this game is built so that typing by hand stays the strongest source of popcorn, and the machines are there so that closing the tab is not a punishment.

Each one you buy makes the next of that kind 18% more expensive, so a wide spread beats stacking one type.

Sixteen Containers

The container is the goal, and filling one hands you a reward worth half its capacity and swaps in a bigger one. S cup, M cup, L cup, mega bucket, oil drum, market cart, dump truck, an entire building, a large mountain, the whole town, the Japanese archipelago, the Earth, the Earth and the Moon together, the solar system, the Milky Way, and then the entire universe.

From the Earth onward the background leaves the ground and goes to space, and the music changes with it — there are five stages of scenery and six tracks, all keyed to which container you are on. And the universe is not the end. Past it each container simply needs eight times what the last one did, forever.

After You Fill the Universe

Clearing the game unlocks a cheat mode that multiplies all production by 100 and lets you run the whole thing again from the top. It exists because the honest version of the endgame is slow, and because watching numbers you spent hours earning become trivial is its own small pleasure.

The Ranking

Enter a name of up to twelve characters and your total lands on a board shared by everyone playing, showing the top 100. It updates itself as you play rather than only at the end, so leaving the game running does count.

Developer's Note

Every keystroke sending popcorn popping just feels great. What starts as a single cupful ends up burying trucks and entire cities in popcorn, and I love that ridiculous sense of scale. The post-clear bonus, especially, is built to crank the satisfaction up even higher.

The decision I went back and forth on longest was keeping the facilities weak. Every idle game I have played eventually stops needing you, and the moment it does I stop opening it. So the machines here are flavour and the hands are the engine. It makes the game a worse idle game and a better typing game, and I would rather have the second one.

FAQ

Can I play on a phone?
It's a keyboard game, so we recommend playing on a PC.
Do I need to read Japanese?
No. Prompts are shown in hiragana with the romaji to type, so you are always copying Latin letters. It works as Japanese typing practice if you are learning, and as plain typing practice if you are not.
Does it keep going while I'm away?
Yes. Once you buy some facilities, they keep producing popcorn automatically even while your hands are off the keyboard.
Why is my combo resetting?
One wrong character sends it to zero. The multiplier tops out at ten times for 200 consecutive correct characters, so accuracy is worth real money here.
Is there an end?
Filling the sixteenth container, the universe, clears the game and unlocks a 100x cheat mode. After that containers keep growing by eight times each, indefinitely.
Will I lose my progress?
It saves to your browser every five seconds. Clearing your browser's site data will erase it, and it does not transfer to another device.
